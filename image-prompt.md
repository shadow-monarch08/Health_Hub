**Prompt:**

> Design a **thoughtful, calm medical-themed header background** for a **“Forgot Password” screen** in a modern healthcare mobile application.
>
> **Emotional Tone & Intent**
>
> * Convey **reassurance, patience, and clarity**, reducing user anxiety during account recovery
> * Calm and empathetic rather than alert or urgent
>
> **Color & Atmosphere**
>
> * Use a **soft, soothing gradient** based on medical teals, gentle blues, and clean whites
> * Smooth color transitions with no harsh contrast or visual noise
>
> **Primary Abstract Element**
>
> * Include a **very subtle, abstract interpretation of a question mark or key**
> * Rendered in a **glassmorphism style**: translucent, softly lit, rounded, and lightly embedded into the background
> * The symbol should feel **suggestive, not literal** — almost discoverable rather than immediately obvious
>
> **Composition & Layout Intent**
>
> * Keep visual interest **off-center (preferably right-weighted)**
> * Maintain **large, clean negative space** across the center and left areas to support text and UI overlays
> * Avoid visual hierarchy conflicts with foreground content
>
> **Style & Quality**
>
> * High-quality, modern healthcare UI aesthetic
> * Subtle depth, soft highlights, gentle blur — no sharp edges or heavy shadows
> * Minimal detail density to ensure long-term visual comfort
>
> **Technical Constraints**
>
> * **4:3 aspect ratio**
> * High resolution, suitable for mobile headers and responsive cropping
> * Background only — **no text, no UI components, no logos**
>
> **Overall Impression**
>
> * Feels supportive and calm
> * Clearly communicates “account recovery” without stress
> * Consistent with premium health and identity-related interfaces

---

### Optional Negative Prompt (Recommended)

> Avoid alarmist visuals, high contrast colors, literal icons, sharp outlines, busy textures, text, branding, or aggressive security symbolism.
